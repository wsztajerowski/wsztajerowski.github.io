What if your identity belonged to you, not to the sites you log in to? Through
Paweł's stories — a messenger without a server, an e-identity, an account at
ssi-Bank, shopping abroad and proving he is old enough to buy a drink — the talk
walks through self-sovereign identity: decentralized identifiers and DID
documents, wallets and DIDComm, verifiable credentials and the trust triangle,
selective disclosure, governance frameworks, the Trust over IP stack, and a
zero-knowledge proof of age built from hash chains.
